# ADR 0005 — AGPL-3.0 license

**Status:** superseded by [ADR 0016](./0016-licencia-propietaria.md), which in turn was
superseded on 2026-10-14 · **Date:** 2026-06-04

> Historical record. The current license is the one in [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE):
> AGPL-3.0-or-later with an additional attribution term (section 7(b)). The reasoning is
> in [`docs/DECISIONES.md`](../DECISIONES.md).

## Context

orgtable is an open-source clone of a SaaS product (Trello). A license has to be chosen.

## Decision

Publish under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**. The full text
is in `LICENSE`.

## Rationale

- The AGPL extends the GPL's copyleft to network-accessible services: whoever offers
  orgtable as a service (even modified) must make its code available to the users.
- This prevents a third party from taking the project and offering it as a closed SaaS
  without contributing back, which fits a self-hosted clone of a SaaS.

## Consequences

- Contributions are licensed under AGPL-3.0.
- License headers/notices must be kept. See `docs/contributing.md`.
- Accepted trade-off: the AGPL may slow down enterprise adoption compared with a
  permissive license (MIT), in exchange for protecting the project's open nature.
